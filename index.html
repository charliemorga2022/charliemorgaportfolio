Case study — Warranty claim process

How a retailer-raised warranty case moves through Salesforce, from submission to approval.

01

Case Raised

Retailer submits a warranty claim in Salesforce, creating a new Case record. A receipt must be attached to the case.

→
02

Review Claim Info

Verify account name, purchase date, model & serial number, and customer name.

→
03

Approval

Claim is approved (or returned for correction) and the record is updated.

Fields checked in step 02

1
Receipt AttachedConfirms proof of purchase is present before the claim proceeds
2
Account NameConfirms the retail partner submitting the claim
3
Date of PurchaseVerifies the tool is still within its warranty window
4
Model NumberIdentifies the exact product covered by the claim
5
Serial NumberMatches the unit to manufacturing and recall records
6
Customer NameTies the claim to the end user for follow-up
